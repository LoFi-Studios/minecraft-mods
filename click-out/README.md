# Click Out

Click outside a container and it closes.

Minecraft already does this if you're on a touchscreen. This lets you do it with a mouse.

That's the whole mod.

## Works on

Every container. Chests, crafting tables, furnaces, your inventory, the creative one, all of them. If it's a container screen, clicking beside it closes it.

Not the pause menu or settings though. Those stay where they are.

## One thing it doesn't do

If you're holding an item stack, it gets out of the way and lets the game handle the click. Clicking outside with a stack is how you drop it, and I didn't want to break that.

## Needs

- Minecraft 26.3
- Fabric Loader 0.20.1 or newer
- Java 25

Client side only. Putting it on a server won't do anything.

## Installing

Drop the jar in your mods folder. Nothing to configure.
